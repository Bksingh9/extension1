## Positioning memo (the angle gap)

The Strait of Hormuz has been a YouTube perennial for ten years —
"what if Iran closed it" — owned by RealLifeLore, Caspian Report,
and Wendover. **Every existing video is now obsolete**: Iran *has*
closed it, since 28 February 2026. The dominant treatments also
cover oil-only; none integrate the **digital chokepoint** dimension
(AAE-1, FALCON, Gulf Bridge cables converging at the same pinch
point) that is now an active threat vector. The Dossier's wedge:
six minutes, oil **and** cables, every number with an EIA / IEA /
TeleGeography source caption on screen, the 28 February timeline
in red, the structural facts in teal, and zero speculation about
what happens next. The viewer leaves with a reusable mental model
of the chokepoint, not a news recap.

## Sourced facts (verified web-stage)

> Tag = visual signal:
>   `[RED]` declassified / official / damning / court-finding
>   `[AMBER]` allegation, contested, ongoing
>   `[TEAL]` cross-checked against primary source

1. **`[TEAL]` The Strait of Hormuz is approximately 34 km wide at its
   narrowest point.** IEA gives 34 km / 21 mi; some sources cite the
   23 nm shipping-lane figure (~43 km). We use **IEA's 34 km** with
   attribution.
   - International Energy Agency (IEA): https://www.iea.org/about/oil-security-and-emergency-response/strait-of-hormuz
   - Wikipedia, *Strait of Hormuz*: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Hormuz

2. **`[TEAL]` In 2024, oil flow through the Strait averaged 20 million
   barrels per day — about 20% of global petroleum-liquids consumption,
   and more than 25% of total global seaborne oil trade.**
   - U.S. EIA, *Today In Energy*, "Amid regional conflict, the Strait
     of Hormuz remains critical oil chokepoint" — primary source.
   - https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=65504

3. **`[TEAL]` Q1 2025 oil flows were essentially flat to 2024 — the
   chokepoint's role had not diminished going into the 2026 crisis.**
   - U.S. EIA — same article as #2.

4. **`[TEAL]` In 2024, Saudi Arabia's crude and condensate exports
   accounted for 38% of total Hormuz crude flows — 5.5 million b/d.**
   - U.S. EIA — same article.

5. **`[TEAL]` Approximately one-fifth of global liquefied natural gas
   trade transited the Strait in 2024, primarily from Qatar.**
   - U.S. EIA, *Today In Energy*: https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=65584

6. **`[TEAL]` Subsea cables passing through or near the Strait include
   Asia-Africa-Europe-1 (AAE-1) — landings in UAE, Oman, Qatar, Saudi
   Arabia; FALCON — Gulf to India / Sri Lanka via Sudan and Egypt;
   Gulf Bridge International — links every Gulf country including Iran.**
   - U.S. News explainer (28 Apr 2026): https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-04-28/explainer-the-hormuz-digital-chokepoint-how-does-the-iran-war-threaten-subsea-cables
   - TeleGeography, *Submarine Cable Map*: https://www.submarinecablemap.com/
   - TeleGeography, *Navigating Hostile Waters: Submarine Cable
     Infrastructure and the Strait of Hormuz*:
     https://resources.telegeography.com/submarine-cable-infrastructure-strait-hormuz

7. **`[TEAL]` All of the cables passing the Strait were laid in Omani
   territorial waters, deliberately routed away from Iranian waters
   given long-standing political risk.**
   - U.S. News explainer (28 Apr 2026), citing TeleGeography.

8. **`[RED]` On 28 February 2026, Israeli strikes — with U.S.
   intelligence support — killed Iran's Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali
   Khamenei in Tehran. Iran confirmed his death on 1 March 2026.**
   - Al Jazeera (28 Feb 2026): https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/2/28/irans-supreme-leader-ali-khamenei-killed-in-us-israeli-attacks-reports
   - NPR (28 Feb 2026): https://www.npr.org/2026/02/28/1123499337/iran-israel-ayatollah-ali-khamenei-killed
   - Wikipedia, *Assassination of Ali Khamenei*: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Ali_Khamenei

9. **`[RED]` Members of Khamenei's family — including his daughter
   Boshra Khamenei, son-in-law Mesbah Bagheri Kani, granddaughter
   Zahra Mohammadi Golpayegani, and daughter-in-law Zahra
   Haddad-Adel — were also killed in the strikes on his compound.**
   - Wikipedia, *Assassination of Ali Khamenei* (cited above).

10. **`[RED]` On 8 March 2026, the Iranian Assembly of Experts elected
    Khamenei's son, Mojtaba Khamenei, as Supreme Leader.**
    - Wikipedia, *2026 Iranian Supreme Leader election*:
      https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iranian_Supreme_Leader_election

11. **`[RED]` Shipping traffic through the Strait of Hormuz has been
    largely blocked by Iran since 28 February 2026, when the
    U.S./Israel air war began.**
    - Wikipedia, *2026 Strait of Hormuz crisis*:
      https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_crisis

12. **`[AMBER]` Iran has publicly threatened the 17 undersea cables
    transiting the Persian Gulf region. As of late April 2026 there
    is no confirmed cable cut attributable to Iran during the crisis.**
    - The Week (28 Mar 2026): https://www.theweek.in/theweek/more/2026/03/28/securing-the-seas-how-irans-cable-threats-highlight-the-need-for-maritime-security.html
    - Iran International (22 Apr 2026): https://www.iranintl.com/en/202604225913

## What this episode will not claim

- Any prediction about how the war ends, how long the closure lasts,
  or what oil/gas prices "will" do. The script states what each
  $1/bbl change historically maps to in retail prices, then stops.
- Any cable cut **attributable to Iran during the 2026 crisis**
  unless we can cite a confirming submarine-cable network operator
  statement. As of writing, threats — yes; confirmed cuts — no.
- Any speculation about U.S. or Israeli rules of engagement.
- Anything about Khamenei's family beyond the names confirmed in
  Wikipedia's footnoted list.
