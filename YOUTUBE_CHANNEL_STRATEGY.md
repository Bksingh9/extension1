## Legal Disclaimers (Important)

All videos must include:
1. "For entertainment and educational purposes only"
2. "All data sourced from public records" (cite Brown University, SIPRI, etc.)
3. "Views expressed are commentary, not financial/political advice"
4. "Always do your own research"

- Defamation claims (everything is publicly sourced facts + opinion)
- Financial advice liability (not giving investment advice)
- Political censorship claims (clearly labeled as commentary)
