# Strategy: Prediction Report Marketplace

## The Business Model

Run MiroFish simulations on **trending, high-interest topics** and sell the resulting prediction reports. Think of it as a "Prediction Newsletter" meets "Research-as-a-Service."

---

## Distribution Channels

### Channel 1: Substack / Newsletter (Primary)
- Free tier: Weekly summary of 1 prediction + behind-the-scenes
- Paid tier ($10-25/month): Full reports, deep dives, early access
- **Why**: Built-in audience growth, recurring revenue, SEO benefits

### Channel 2: Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy (Individual Reports)
- One-off reports: $15-75 each
- Report bundles: $99-299 for themed packs
- **Why**: No subscription commitment, impulse purchases

### Channel 3: Twitter/X Thread + Report Upsell
- Post engaging simulation highlights as threads
- Link to full report at the end
- **Why**: Viral potential, free marketing

### Channel 4: LinkedIn Articles (B2B)
- Publish executive summaries targeting business decision-makers
- Upsell full reports and consulting calls
- **Why**: High-value professional audience

---

## Hot Topic Categories (What Sells)

### Category 1: Elections & Politics (Highest Demand)
- Election outcome simulations (local, state, national)
- Policy impact predictions (tax changes, regulations)
- Public reaction to political events
- **Timing**: 6-12 months before any major election
- **Price**: $25-75/report

### Category 2: Market & Economy
- "What if [major company] does X?" scenarios
- Tariff and trade war impact simulations
- Industry disruption predictions (AI replacing jobs, new tech adoption)
- **Timing**: React to breaking financial news within 48 hours
- **Price**: $30-100/report

### Category 3: Tech & AI Trends
- "What happens when AGI arrives?" type scenarios
- Social media platform migration simulations
- Adoption curves for new technologies
- **Timing**: Evergreen + reactive to major announcements
- **Price**: $15-50/report

### Category 4: Culture & Social
- Celebrity/brand crisis simulations
- Social movement trajectory predictions
- Consumer behavior shift modeling
- **Timing**: Reactive -- jump on trending topics fast
- **Price**: $10-30/report

### Category 5: Sports & Entertainment
- Season outcome simulations
- "What if [team] trades [player]?" scenarios
- Box office / streaming performance predictions
- **Timing**: Seasonal -- pre-season and before major events
- **Price**: $10-25/report

## Content Calendar (First Month)

### Week 1: Launch Week
- **Monday**: Free report on a hot trending topic (builds credibility)
- **Wednesday**: Twitter thread with key findings + link to full report
- **Friday**: LinkedIn article with B2B angle on the same topic

### Week 2: Build Momentum
- **Monday**: Paid report #1 (choose high-demand topic)
- **Wednesday**: Free mini-report (teaser for a bigger topic)
- **Friday**: "Behind the simulation" content showing how you do it

### Week 3: Audience Growth
- **Monday**: Paid report #2
- **Wednesday**: Respond to a breaking news event with a quick simulation
- **Friday**: Comparison report -- "Our simulation vs what actually happened" (builds trust)

### Week 4: Optimize
- **Monday**: Paid report #3
- **Wednesday**: Audience poll -- "What should I simulate next?" (engagement)
- **Friday**: Monthly wrap-up + preview of next month's topics
