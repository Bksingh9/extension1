## Channel-level competitors (the lanes we have to beat)

### 1. ColdFusion (Dagogo Altraide)
- **Positioning:** Calm, music-driven explainers on tech, history, business; cinematic visuals.
- **Format:** ~10–20 min, talking voiceover over stock + custom motion graphics.
- **Cadence:** Roughly bi-weekly.
- **Strengths:** Trust, voice, mood. The reference for "calm + serious" on YouTube.
- **Gaps for us:** Rarely cites primary sources on screen; mostly tech/business, light on intel/geopolitics deep dives.

### 2. Johnny Harris
- **Positioning:** Map-driven geopolitics, US foreign policy, dark history.
- **Format:** 12–25 min, animated maps, on-camera presenter.
- **Cadence:** Weekly-ish.
- **Strengths:** Map animation, narrative arc, hook-craft.
- **Gaps for us:** On-camera face is the brand; sourcing visible only in description (sometimes contested). The Dossier is faceless and document-first.

### 3. RealLifeLore
- **Positioning:** Geopolitics, economics, "what if" scenarios.
- **Format:** 15–30 min, animated maps + chart inserts.
- **Cadence:** Weekly.
- **Strengths:** Reach (multi-million subs), evergreen titles.
- **Gaps for us:** Speculative framing; less interest in declassified docs / court records.

### 4. Wendover Productions
- **Positioning:** Logistics, infrastructure, geopolitics — engineering-flavoured.
- **Format:** 10–20 min, motion graphics + maps.
- **Cadence:** Weekly.
- **Strengths:** Crisp explainers; great at making infrastructure visible.
- **Gaps for us:** Very rarely touches intelligence ops, financial crime, or contested history.

### 5. Empire Files / Kim Iversen / Bellingcat (channel) / Caspian Report
- **Positioning:** Investigative + geopolitics, varying degrees of opinion vs. analysis.
- **Format:** Mixed — talking-head + clip cuts; document-driven on Bellingcat.
- **Strengths:** Deep into the dark-world beat already; Bellingcat especially is the gold standard for OSINT sourcing.
- **Gaps for us:** None of them do *animated*, voice-over-only, 5–7 minute explainers with on-screen colour-coded source signalling. **That's our wedge.**

## What this means for The Dossier
- **Voice rule:** stay calm. The market for "shouty conspiracy" is saturated and demonetised.
- **Visual rule:** every numerical claim has a source caption on screen. Make the citations the visual signature.
- **Length rule:** 5–7 min maximum. The market for 2-hour Gladio docs is owned by Francovich; we are not competing there.
- **Cadence rule:** weekly after the pilot debugs the pipeline. By video 4, the format is recognisable.
