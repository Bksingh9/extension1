## Findings

### 5–8 emerging themes

#### T1 — The Hormuz crisis is no longer hypothetical
Iran has blockaded the Strait since **28 February 2026**, after the
US/Israel air war and the assassination of Khamenei. ~20 M barrels/day
of seaborne oil and the AAE-1, FALCON, and Gulf Bridge cables now sit
in the same chokepoint. The "what if Iran closed Hormuz" speculative
genre that owns YouTube is now **out of date**.

**Angle for The Dossier:** *"The Day Iran Closed the Strait — what
the chokepoint now means for oil **and** the internet."* Merges
candidate #5 (Hormuz oil) and #10 (undersea cables) into one piece.

#### T2 — MK-ULTRA's Korean War chapter just got declassified
The Intercept (26 April 2026) and the National Security Archive's
"CIA and the Behavioral Sciences" collection (1,200+ records) confirm
MK-ULTRA experiments on North Korean POWs in US custody. This is
**new documentary evidence** on a topic the audience already knows
the name of.

**Angle:** *"MK-ULTRA in the Camps — what the 2026 declassification
adds to the 20,000 pages we already had."* Slots into candidate #6
with a fresh news peg.

#### T3 — Lazarus is breaking its own records
$577 M stolen in the first four months of 2026 alone (Drift Protocol
$285 M on 1 April; KelpDAO $292 M on 18 April). North Korea is now
**76 %** of all global crypto-hack value YTD; cumulative attributed
theft since 2017 is $6–7.5 B depending on source.

**Angle:** *"How a Bankrupt State Stole $7 Billion in Crypto — and
why 2026 is the worst year yet."* Slots into candidate #8 with the
freshest possible peg.

#### T4 — Wirecard is finally producing convictions
Singapore (Jan 2026) sentenced R. Shanmugaratnam to 10 years and
J. H. O'Sullivan to 6.5 years for falsifying Wirecard accounts. The
Munich trial of Markus Braun is in its fourth year and 260+ sessions.
Marsalek is now suspected (Nov 2025) in a separate €300 M
"Operation Chargeback" fraud.

**Angle:** *"The Wirecard Map — every conviction, every still-missing
person, six years after the €1.9 B vanished."* Visual: an animated
org chart that fills in arrest-by-arrest, with empty boxes for the
fugitives. Slots into candidate #4.

#### T5 — Baltic + Hormuz cable cuts are now a continuous beat
Fitburg seized in Finland on 31 December 2025, dragging anchor over
the Helsinki-Tallinn cable. Iran threatened the 17 Hormuz cables in
March 2026. This is **a recurring news pattern**, not a one-off.

**Angle:** *"1.4 Million Kilometres of Wire — every cable that's been
cut since November 2024."* A timeline-and-map piece. Slots into
candidate #10. Risk: heavy overlap with T1's cable section if both
are made — pick one.

#### T6 — Stasi/Chernobyl files just opened (40th anniversary)
Newly declassified Stasi files (released around the 40th anniversary,
April–May 2026) show KGB and Stasi knew the Chernobyl explosion was
catastrophic and prioritised press handling over public health.
Anniversary peg + new document = ideal Dossier setup.

**Angle:** *"What the Stasi Knew About Chernobyl — and didn't tell."*
Not in the original candidate list. Cinematic. Animation leverage:
medium (timeline + redacted documents).

#### T7 — JFK files: CIA-Mexico ops (operator-fill freshness check)
National Security Archive published 80,000 pages (May 2025) on the
CIA's Mexico City Station and joint operations with the Mexican
government. A year old now — the news cycle has moved past it, but
the documents are still under-explored on YouTube.

**Angle:** *"The Mexico City Station — what the JFK files reveal
about the CIA's longest-running joint operation."* Lower urgency than
T1–T3.

#### T8 — Soviet dissolution, 35-year anniversary (December 2026)
On 26 December 2026, the USSR dissolution will be 35 years old.
Lead-time consideration only — too far out for the May–June slate,
but worth holding a slot for November–December.

**Angle (parked):** *"December 25th, 1991 — the 90 minutes between
the flag coming down and the codes changing hands."* This belongs
more to The Long Light's voice than The Dossier's. See that scan.

### Top 2 video angles

#### **#1 — "The Day Iran Closed the Strait" (T1)**
The pilot (Operation Gladio) ships first. T1 is the **second
episode** target — a live, high-demand story where The Dossier's
on-screen citations + animated maps + chokepoint visualisation are
exactly the format the news cycle is missing. Risk: the war is
still active; the script must be designed to age well (focus on
why the chokepoint matters, not the day-by-day).

#### **#2 — "MK-ULTRA in the Camps" (T2)**
A textbook Dossier piece: famous-name topic, brand-new primary
documents (1,200+ records), short enough to do in 6 minutes, and
the MK-ULTRA brand is search-evergreen. Lower live-news risk than
T1, so it's the safer follow-up if T1 slips.

#### Hold for slot 4–6
T3 (Lazarus) and T4 (Wirecard) are both 17-point alternates. T3 has
edge on visual leverage (Sankey diagrams of crypto flows); T4 has
edge on the moat (court records are the canonical Dossier source).
Pick whichever has the cleaner primary-source set when its slot
comes up.
