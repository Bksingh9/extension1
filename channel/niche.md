# Niche strategy — The Dossier

## Audience
Curious adults (25–44, global English) who follow ColdFusion, Johnny Harris, RealLifeLore, Wendover, and Empire Files — but want primary-source citations on every claim, not vibes.

## Promise
Every video walks through one piece of the dark world — declassified intel ops, financial crimes, geopolitical chokepoints — built from public documents and primary sources, with every claim cited in the description.

## Voice & tone
**Measured. Forensic. Cinematic.** See `channel/brand.md` for the sample read and voice rules.

## Editorial guardrails (the moat)
- Every fact has a footnote. No claim makes it into a script without a source URL in `episodes/NNN/research.md`.
- Clear visual signal between **verified** (teal), **alleged** (amber), and **damning** (red).
- We cover: declassified ops, court-record financial crime, geopolitical analysis grounded in primary data.
- We do not cover: election denial, anti-vaccine claims, ethnic / religious replacement theories, mass-shooting denial, Holocaust revisionism, or any claim we cannot source to credible primary material. The brand dies the day we slip on this.

## Top 10 candidate topics (ranked by demand-to-supply gap)

1. **Operation Gladio: NATO's Secret War Inside Its Own Borders** — *Hook:* In 1990, an Italian Prime Minister admitted his government had run a secret army for 45 years. Fourteen NATO countries had one too. **(PILOT)**

2. **Operation Northwoods: The False-Flag the Pentagon Wrote Down** — *Hook:* In 1962, the U.S. Joint Chiefs proposed staging terrorist attacks on Americans to justify invading Cuba. They put it in writing. JFK said no.

3. **The Phoenix Program: Vietnam's Approved Assassination Bureau** — *Hook:* The CIA killed more people in Vietnam in three years than any battle of the war. The paperwork survives.

4. **Wirecard: How €1.9 Billion Vanished and the Regulator Looked Away** — *Hook:* A German fintech became more valuable than Deutsche Bank. The cash on its balance sheet didn't exist. The regulator was investigating the journalists.

5. **The Strait of Hormuz: 21% of the World's Oil Through 33 km of Water** — *Hook:* If Iran closed the Strait tomorrow, gas prices double in three weeks. Here's why nobody stops them.

6. **MKUltra: What's Actually in the 20,000 Pages** — *Hook:* Everyone has heard of MKUltra. Almost nobody has read the documents. Today we read the documents.

7. **The Panama Papers: 11.5 Million Documents, Visualized** — *Hook:* Twelve heads of state. Sixty more linked. One law firm in Panama City. Here is how the money actually moved.

8. **Lazarus Group: How a Bankrupt State Stole $3 Billion in Crypto** — *Hook:* North Korea is sanctioned to economic death. Last year it stole more crypto than any criminal organization on Earth.

9. **The Lost Decade: How Mexico Became a Narco-State Without the Word Being Said** — *Hook:* Mexico has had 400,000 murders since 2006. The cartels did not beat the state. They became part of it.

10. **Undersea Cables: 99% of the Internet Runs on a Wire You Can Cut With an Anchor** — *Hook:* 1.4 million kilometres of fibre on the seafloor. Russia maps it. The NSA taps it. The Houthis cut it.

## Visual identity
- **Primary accent:** `#d63b2f` (redaction red)
- **Supporting:** `#e3a72f` (archive amber) and `#3aa7a0` (verified teal)
- **Font pairing:** Inter (titles + body) + JetBrains Mono (document overlays)
- **Recurring motifs:** manila-folder framing, classified/declassified stamps, animated maps with cell-pin reveals, money-flow Sankey diagrams, redaction bars on alleged-but-unproven names.

## Cadence & format
- **Length:** 5–7 minutes for the first 12 episodes.
- **Cadence:** Weekly after the pilot debugs the pipeline.
- **Pilot target:** ship within 14 days.

## What "winning" looks like at 90 days
12 videos shipped, 1k subs, one breakout video at >10× average views. By video 10, `shared/styles.py` has earned its keep and Episode 11 takes half the time of Episode 1.
