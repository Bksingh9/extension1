## Channel-level competitors

### 1. Johnny Harris (primary reference)
- **Positioning:** Documentary filmmaker turned YouTuber. Geopolitics + history + personal travelogue.
- **Format:** 12–25 min. On-camera presenter. Animated maps, archival overlays, music-led emotional swells.
- **Cadence:** ~weekly, sometimes longer for big pieces.
- **Strengths:** Pacing. Music selection. Narrative tension. Thumbnail craft. The single best storyteller in modern YouTube long-form.
- **What we keep:** The cold open, the slow build, the music-led swell, the map/archive aesthetic, the title formulas.
- **What we drop:** His face. The first-person travelogue ("I went to..."). Our wedge: **stories Harris can't go to** — sealed records, dead defectors, lost places, decommissioned facilities.

### 2. ColdFusion (Dagogo Altraide)
- **Positioning:** Tech and business documentaries. Calm voiceover, no presenter.
- **Format:** 10–20 min. Voiceover-led. Motion graphics + stock + custom graphics. Heavy ambient music.
- **Strengths:** Voice. Mood. Polish. The faceless template most channels imitate.
- **Why noted:** Closest existing template for *what we sound like*. Our subject matter differs (intelligence/history vs tech/business), our visual identity differs (cinematic-archival vs futuristic-tech).

### 3. Storied (PBS Digital Studios)
- **Positioning:** Mythology, folklore, history. Calm narrator.
- **Format:** 10–15 min. Voiceover + B-roll. Strong episode arcs.
- **Why noted:** Discipline of the end card and the single-arc structure. Lighter content than ours; the format itself is instructive.

### 4. Real Stories
- **Positioning:** Re-uploads of full broadcast documentaries.
- **Length:** 45+ min.
- **Why noted:** The audience overlap is enormous. People who watch 45-min documentaries will watch our 10-min ones — *if* the storytelling holds. This sets the bar.

### 5. Vox Atlas
- **Positioning:** Map-driven explainer, news-adjacent.
- **Format:** 5–10 min. Animated maps + presenter voiceover.
- **Why noted:** Closer to The Dossier's lane than ours, but the map-animation craft is the gold standard. Worth studying for visual patterns.

## The angle gap

There is no faceless YouTube channel doing **10-minute, single-subject narrative documentaries about declassified-but-still-quiet stories** with primary-source citations and Johnny Harris-grade pacing.

The closest is ColdFusion (right tone, wrong subject), Johnny Harris (right subject and pacing, has a face), and Storied (right format, lighter content). The gap is real and persistent — none of these channels have moved into it in five years of trying.
