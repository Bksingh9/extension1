# The Long Light — Brand

## Identity
- **Channel name:** The Long Light
- **Tagline:** *Stories told slowly.*
- **Recurring framing device:**
  - Open: black → warm light fades in from one edge → cold open on a single moment
  - Pivot: *"This is [scene]. Today, we're going to tell its story."*
  - Close: *"…and that's where we leave it. For now."*
- **Logo concept:** A horizontal beam of warm light fading right-to-left across a dark panel. Subtle photographic gradient. No serif flourishes.

## Voice (3 adjectives)
**Patient. Cinematic. Empathetic.**

Sample paragraph (read aloud, ~25s):
> *"It is a quiet evening in 1991. The sun is setting over a dusty airfield outside Kyiv. A single Tu-134 sits on the tarmac. The cockpit is dark. The pilot has been told to wait. He doesn't know why. By the time he taxis again, in seventeen minutes, his country will not exist. He doesn't know that either. This is The Long Light. Today, we're going to tell his story."*

Voice rules:
- One sentence, one image. Build the picture before the argument.
- Names of people. Names of places. Specific dates. Never vague.
- Short paragraphs. Long sentences allowed when they earn their length.
- Numbers when they mean something. Not as filler.
- Never use *"shocking"*, *"insane"*, *"you won't believe"*, *"this changed everything"*.
- First person rare — *"we"* instead of *"I"*. *"Let's go back to..."* instead of *"I want to tell you about..."*.
- One contraction per minute, max.

## Visual identity
Source of truth: `the-long-light/styles.py` (added when we build the first scene).

| Slot | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| `BG`        | `#0a0e1a` | Deep navy-black. Slightly warmer than Dossier's flat black. |
| `FG`        | `#f5e8d3` | Warm cream — body text, primary lines. |
| `MUTED`     | `#7a8294` | Cool grey — secondary text, citations. |
| `ACCENT`    | `#ffb627` | Signal yellow — brand mark, key reveals, *"the moment"*. |
| `WARM`      | `#d96e3b` | Burnt orange — the human thread (a person, a body, a face). |
| `COOL`      | `#4a6e85` | Slate blue — the system, the place, atmospheric. |
| `INK`       | `#000000` | True black — for archival document overlays only. |

**Color grammar:**
- **Yellow** = the moment we want you to remember
- **Orange** = the human thread
- **Blue** = the system, the place, the surrounding context
- **Cream + dark** = the canvas

## Typography
- **Title + body:** Source Serif Pro (cinematic serif). Fallback: Charter, Georgia.
- **Subtitles + UI:** Inter. Fallback: Helvetica, Arial.
- **Mono / archival:** JetBrains Mono. Fallback: IBM Plex Mono, Menlo.

## Visual motifs
- **Cold-open framing** — first 4 seconds black, warm light fades in from one edge.
- **Single-subject focus** — most frames hold one person, one object, or one place. Crowd shots are rare and earn their place.
- **Vintage paper / archive textures** behind any document overlay.
- **Sepia-toned animated maps** with aerial-photograph aesthetic, not flat-vector.
- **Light-leak + film-grain transitions** between acts.
- **End-card** — a dim long-light bar fades to black under the close line.

## Channel description (long version, ~600 chars — for the YouTube About box)
> A patient, cinematic documentary channel about small moments inside the world's biggest secrets.
>
> Each episode walks slowly through one true story from the dark world — a defector, a decommissioned facility, a sealed file, a lost recording — built around place, time, and the people who were in the room. Every fact is sourced; sources are in the description.
>
> No on-camera presenter. No sponsorship reads. No filler. New episode every 10 days.
